About Me

The first game I played was Call of Duty Modern Warfare 2 on the PS3. Since then I have been hooked. I collect platinum trophies for games which is both rewarding and quite challenging. It's fascinating seeing how games change as you invest more time into them and also how designers create challenges to prolong the life of a game.

I like a variety of games, but my favourite types are big sprawling RPGs like Mass Effect. I also love discovering quirky indie games. It’s my dream to be able to work on something like Pathologic 2. Being a lead game designed is my ultimate ambition. To achieve this I want to learn everything I can about all aspects of game design from the psychology of level design to how to properly utilise sound to evoke emotions in players.

Music is another lifelong passion. I play piano, particularly classical music. I'm working on memorising a collection of pieces at the moment so I can string them together in a long continuous piece.

In my spare time, I am a cinephile. The film I have watched and enjoyed the most is Christopher Nolan’s Dark Knight. It’s interesting to see different techniques that are employed to create atmosphere and the overlaps in story telling between the mediums of cinema and video games. Sometimes though, I just like to get lost in a trashy horror :D
